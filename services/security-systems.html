Smart Camera Mounting & Setup

You provide the cameras and gear — we mount, connect, and configure everything so live view and recordings work on your phone before we leave.

We install and set up customer-supplied equipment. We do not sell or install licensed alarm or CCTV systems.

What's included

  • Video doorbells — wired and battery options you provide
  • Outdoor dome and bullet camera mounting
  • NVR or app-based systems — setup and testing
  • Clean mounting and tidy cable routing at each location
  • App setup, testing, and walkthrough before we leave

Cover the whole property

Driveway, entries, side yards, and backyard — we help plan camera placement for the views that matter and mount your gear with a clean finish at each spot.

  • Front door and driveway coverage
  • Side gates and blind spots
  • Pool, patio, and rear yard
  • Live view and recordings on your phone

See everything in one place

We configure your system so all cameras feed into one dashboard — on a monitor at home, in the app, or both. You leave knowing it works.

Common questions

What camera brands do you set up?

Ring, Nest, Reolink, Ubiquiti, and other quality brands — WiFi, PoE, or battery, depending on the gear you provide.

Can I view cameras on my phone?

Yes. We set up and test the app with you before we leave.

Do you install alarm systems?

No — we focus on mounting and configuring smart cameras and doorbells you already own or purchase separately.
